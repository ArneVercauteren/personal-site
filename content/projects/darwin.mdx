**astralanx** applies genetic programming to stock-picking: it generates candidate
strategies, backtests them, keeps and recombines the best, and repeats this over
many generations.

## What a "strategy" is

A strategy here is neither a hand-written rule nor a neural network. Each one is a
small **abstract syntax tree** built from a domain-specific language of financial
primitives — indicators, comparisons, arithmetic, conditionals, exit rules,
position sizing, and a rebalance interval. The tree compiles to a native
evaluator and runs across the US equity universe.

## How the search works

1. **Generate** a population of random strategy trees.
2. **Backtest** each one (see [the methodology](/astralanx)).
3. **Score** on risk-adjusted return, regime stability, parsimony, and
   originality versus the rest of the population.
4. **Select, mutate, and breed** the best; migrate between parallel islands.
5. After many generations, crown **kings** per objective (balanced, high-Sharpe,
   robust, low-volatility, …).

## From engine to live site

A chosen king is deployed to the [live dashboard](/astralanx/live): its formula
produces target weights that rebalance every one to two months, and a simulated
portfolio paper-trades those weights daily. The strategies whose formulas stay
private show their performance and aggregate sector exposure only; one or two
fully open strategies show everything, end to end.
